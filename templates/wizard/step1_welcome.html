Welcome to Remittance Reconciliation

Let's get you set up in about 5–10 minutes.

What this tool does

Each day you upload a CSV of the previous day's bank deposit report. The tool automatically searches your Microsoft 365 email for remittance documents matching each deposit, saves them as organised PDFs, and keeps a carry-forward queue for anything it couldn't match yet.
